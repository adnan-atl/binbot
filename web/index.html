FAQs

How does {{BRAND}} know my bin days?

It reads your council's own website, using your postcode or address. It checks it again every week, and more often before bank holidays.

Does it work for my council?

It covers most councils in the UK. If yours isn't covered, you can add your bin days yourself and still get reminders.

How much does it cost?

Nothing. {{BRAND}} is free for everyone, and included with {{PARENT_NAME}} membership.

What if my collection day changes?

{{BRAND}} spots changes on your council's calendar, such as bank holiday moves, and messages you with the new date.

The days look wrong for my home.

Reply WRONG to a reminder, or tap "These aren't my days" when you check your bins. {{BRAND}} then looks up your home on its own.

Can I change my reminder time or bins?

Yes. Tap Settings on any reminder, or message {{BRAND}} "settings", and pick a new time or turn bins on and off.

How do I stop the messages?

Reply STOP. Reply START to turn them back on.

What do you do with my data?

We use your address to find your bin days and your WhatsApp number to send your reminders. We never sell your data or share it for third-party marketing. See our privacy policy.
